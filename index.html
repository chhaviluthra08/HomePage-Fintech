FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SecurePay and how does it work? +
SecurePay is a unified digital payment solution that allows businesses to accept, process, and manage transactions securely. It connects merchants to multiple payment channels through our gateway, ensuring fast settlements, encrypted data flow, and real-time reporting — for startups to enterprises.
What payment methods are supported? +
SecurePay supports UPI, credit and debit cards, wallets, net banking, QR code payments, and in-store payment modes via POS and Soundbox — any payment type, online or offline, from a single dashboard.
How long does onboarding take? +
Onboarding is typically completed within 24–48 hours after successful document verification. Required documents include PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, and business information (if applicable).
Are there setup or monthly fees? +
No. You only pay per transaction. There are no setup fees, no monthly minimums, and no hidden costs. Transaction charges vary by payment method and are communicated transparently during onboarding.
When will I receive my settlements? +
Settlements are processed daily and typically credited within 24 hours. Minor delays may occur on weekends or bank holidays, as per banking partner policies.
Is SecurePay RBI & PCI DSS compliant? +
Yes. SecurePay is PCI-DSS compliant and operates in full alignment with applicable RBI and NPCI guidelines. All transaction data is encrypted and processed according to regulatory standards.
Does SecurePay support recurring payments? +
Yes. With Auto Collect and smart payment identifiers, you can automate and schedule recurring collections — reducing manual follow-ups and improving cash flow.
Is there a sandbox environment for developers? +
Yes. Our Dev Studio provides an interactive sandbox where developers can simulate transactions, test edge cases, and validate integrations fully before going live.
